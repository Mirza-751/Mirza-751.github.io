Laraib Mustafa Mirza

M.Sc. Astrophysics & Cosmology, University of Padua (2026). Applying for PhD positions in high-energy astrophysics.

I work in high-energy astrophysics. I like to study radiative processes and understand nature's most extreme particle accelerators. I am interested in how supermassive black holes evolved in the early universe, and in the differences between leptonic and hadronic jet models. For my master's thesis I modelled the broadband spectrum of the z = 3.65 blazar MG3 J163554+3629 with a special focus on the NuSTAR band.
