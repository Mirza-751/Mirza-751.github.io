Qatar-1b transit analysis

Reduced 1,857 frames from the Asiago 1.82 m telescope and added TESS sectors, then fitted the transit with MCMC (BATMAN): P = 1.4391 d, Rp/Rs = 0.1270, i = 84.09°.

Supernova kicks in the Milky Way

N-body simulation of 10⁴ massive stars in a Galactic potential (fireworks + SEVN), comparing natal-kick models across metallicities. Neutron stars end up more widely spread than black holes. Presented at the MW-Gaia School, Vilnius.
